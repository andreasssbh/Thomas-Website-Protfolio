About Thomas

A physiotherapist devoted to precise, personal care.

Originally trained between France, Spain and the United States, Thomas built his practice around one idea: recovery works best when it's personal.

Thomas is a fully qualified kinésithérapeute (physiotherapist), a graduate of Universidad Europea in Madrid, where he completed his degree in physiotherapy between 2018 and 2024. His clinical training took him through hospitals and clinics across Spain and France — working in respiratory rehabilitation, neurological recovery, and post-operative care.

Today, Thomas practices as an independent physiotherapist (kinésithérapeute libéral) in Saint-Tropez, seeing patients both in clinic and at home. Whether it's a professional athlete recovering from injury, a post-surgery patient regaining mobility, or someone simply looking to move better, he brings the same attention to detail to every session.

Services

Care built around how you actually live.

Every treatment plan is tailored to the person in front of him — not a generic protocol. In clinic or at home, the approach stays the same: precise, attentive, and results-driven.

01

Orthopedic & Musculoskeletal

Rehabilitation for joint, muscle and tendon injuries — from sprains and strains to post-fracture recovery.

02

Post-Operative Recovery

Structured, progressive rehabilitation after surgery, focused on restoring mobility and strength safely.

03

Respiratory Therapy

Cardiorespiratory rehabilitation and airway clearance techniques for respiratory conditions and recovery.

04

Neurological Rehabilitation

Support for patients recovering from stroke, spinal cord injury, and other neurological conditions.

05

Sports Recovery & Performance

Injury prevention, recovery and reconditioning programs built for athletes and active lifestyles.

06

At-Home Visits

Full sessions brought directly to you — ideal for patients with limited mobility or a packed schedule.
